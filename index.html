HOW IT WORKS

What the model values

1

Route-aware wind. Meteorological wind direction is converted to a wind vector, then projected along every segment bearing.

2

Cycling physics. For each route leg, speed is solved from gravity, rolling resistance and relative-air aerodynamic drag.

3

PR filters. Only daylight windows are ranked; rain and very high gusts are penalized or removed.

4

Forecast honesty. Tomorrow's forecast is more trustworthy than day seven, so horizon confidence is shown separately from speed.
